Data flow

Where your data goes, and where it doesn't

1 · Capture

PiC sensors

Existing Intelligent Counting sensors observe movement between zones on your premises.

2 · Mask & analyse

Edge GPU (on site)

Faces and heads are masked and movement is analysed locally. No facial recognition is performed.

3 · Output

Counts + anonymous IDs

A lightweight XML file carries zone, direction, timestamp and an anonymous ID. No images.

4 · Report

Chord, Sankey, Timeline

Intelligent Counting software turns the data into journey reporting for your team.

✕ No raw video stored ✕ No video leaves the building ✕ No face templates or biometric data ✕ No cloud video processing ✕ No names, accounts or device tracking

For tenders & procurement

Ten questions to ask any journey-tracking vendor

Whoever you choose, get these answered in writing. We're happy to answer every one for Orbit.

  1. Is any facial recognition or biometric template created, even temporarily?
  2. Where is video analysed: on site, or in a cloud region?
  3. Is any image or video leaving my network? To whom and where?
  4. Are faces masked before storage or only after?
  5. How long do visitor identifiers persist, and when do they reset?
  6. Can the identifiers be linked to a person, device or account?
  7. Will you support a DPIA and provide signage and legitimate-interest guidance?
  8. Who is controller and who is processor, and is a DPA provided?
  9. Which sub-processors and international transfers are involved?
  10. Can you support data subject requests (access, erasure, objection)?

Frequently asked questions

Is Orbit GDPR compliant?

Orbit is built to support GDPR compliance through data minimisation, on-device processing and no facial recognition. Compliance ultimately depends on how it is deployed, so as controller you should complete a DPIA. We provide documentation to make that straightforward.

Do we need to display signage?

Transparency is a core GDPR requirement. We recommend clear signage explaining that anonymous movement analytics are in use, and we can provide suggested wording.

Can anyone be identified from Orbit data?

Faces are masked, no biometric templates are created, and IDs are random and not linked to names, devices or accounts. The output cannot be used to recognise an individual. CONFIRM against technical team

Does any data leave our site?

Video analysis happens entirely on the on-site edge GPU. Only the lightweight XML output (counts and anonymous IDs) is used by reporting software. CONFIRM: where reporting is hosted

What legal basis should we rely on?

Many venues rely on legitimate interests for operational analytics, supported by a balancing test. That decision belongs to you and your DPO. We provide a template assessment to help.
